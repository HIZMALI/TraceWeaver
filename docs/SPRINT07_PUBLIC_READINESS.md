## Scope and privacy

The staging exporter selects the wheel, runtime dependency wheels, exact synthetic judge bundle,
lock, notices, launcher and these guides. No user store, models, FFmpeg executable, cache, provider
logs or recordings are exported by that script. The synthetic judge `.rl` is the sole allowed store.
Wheels contain native platform libraries from dependencies; they are expected runtime artifacts,
not portable source. Local Windows/Python 3.14 wheels do not prove Linux or Python 3.11 support.

Pattern screening of tracked/staged files is limited and cannot guarantee absence of secrets.
Historical evidence may contain local example paths; inspect the final screening scope and counts.
Do not publish ignored verification trees or native installation logs. The citation firewall does not
authenticate source truth. No production incidents, remediation or causal inference are claimed.

The earlier graph-versus-flat study has only 13 valid questions under its documented correction;
there is no universal retrieval-superiority or AI-accuracy claim. Frozen gold was not relabeled.
